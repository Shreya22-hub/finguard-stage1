FinGuard Qwen3-0.6B experiment.
Check metrics.csv and results.json for the model comparison.
Plots include unsafe F1, accuracy, precision/recall, confusion matrices,
and self-play RL rollout diagnostics.
The mini self-play RL stage is a small GRPO-inspired experiment,
not the original paper's full-scale GRPO reproduction.
The 16-pair compliance test and 200-example injection test are different
tasks and are not directly comparable to the paper benchmark.
